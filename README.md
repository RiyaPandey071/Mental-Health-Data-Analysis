## Project Overview

This project focuses on **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** of a Mental Health Survey dataset collected from employees working in the technology industry. The objective is to understand the factors affecting mental health in the workplace by analyzing treatment-seeking behavior, workplace support, family history, work interference, and organizational resources. Through data cleaning, statistical analysis, and visualization, the project uncovers patterns that help better understand workplace mental health.

## Analysis & Insights

### Demographic Analysis

- Analyzed employee distribution based on age and gender.
- Identified common age groups participating in the survey.

### Mental Health Treatment Analysis

- Examined the proportion of employees who sought mental health treatment.
- Compared treatment behavior across different employee groups.

### Family History Analysis

- Studied the relationship between family history of mental illness and treatment-seeking behavior.
- Observed that employees with a family history were generally more likely to seek treatment.

### Workplace Support Analysis

- Evaluated the availability of mental health resources in organizations.
- Compared employee willingness to discuss mental health issues with supervisors and coworkers.
- Analyzed employer attitudes toward mental health awareness.

### Work Interference Analysis

- Investigated how mental health affected employees' work performance.
- Compared levels of work interference among different groups.

### Correlation & Statistical Analysis

- Performed correlation analysis to identify relationships among selected variables.
- Applied basic statistical techniques to understand trends and support observations.

## Summary

This project demonstrates how Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) can be used to better understand workplace mental health using survey data. By cleaning, analyzing, and visualizing the dataset, the project identifies important factors such as family history, workplace support, treatment behavior, and work interference that influence employees' mental well-being. The analysis provides meaningful insights that can support organizations in creating healthier work environments.

---

## Conclusion

The analysis indicates that workplace support and organizational awareness play an important role in encouraging employees to seek mental health treatment. Employees with a family history of mental illness were more likely to receive treatment, while workplace culture significantly influenced employees' willingness to discuss mental health concerns. Overall, the project highlights the importance of mental health awareness, supportive work environments, and data-driven decision-making for improving employee well-being.
